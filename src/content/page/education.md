I am a lifelong learner. Here is a summary of my formal education and academic achievements.

## Papers / Conferences

- Presented several times to Tableau User Group in Sydney.
- Presented to R-Ladies Sydney.
- Presented at conferences including Microsoft Architecture Council, ACS NSW Branch Forum, and AUUG'93 and AUUG'94.
- Presented ["Services Oriented Architecture: opportunities and challenges of embedding SOA in a Retail Bank"](/cv/article/banktech-2006/) at Bank.Tech.06 (Sydney, 27–28 July 2006), while Head of Architecture, Technology Australia, Retail Banking at NAB, with the [original deck](/cv/Banktech06%20NAB%20Chris%20Tham%20SOA.ppt).
- Presented "Distributed Client/Server Architectures and Transaction Processing on Open Systems" at [AUUG'94](/cv/article/auug-1994/) and, in revised form, at [Oracle OpenWorld '94](/cv/article/openworld-1994/), while at AT&T Global Information Solutions, with the Word manuscripts for [AUUG'94](/cv/DCSATP.DOC) and [OpenWorld](/cv/C_THAM.DOC).
- Presented ["Performance Tuning for Relational Database Applications"](/cv/article/auug-1993/) at AUUG'93, while at NCR Australia, with the [Word manuscript](/cv/AUUG.DOC).
- Co-authored technical paper ["UNIX Password Encryption considered insecure"](/cv/article/crypt-usenix91/) (USENIX Winter '91 Conference, Dallas, pp. 269–279), with a [scan of the proceedings](/cv/crypt-usenix91.pdf).
- Developed a method for distributed/parallel computation of options risk management, co-authored as ["Distributed Computing & Risk Management"](/cv/article/suntech-1990/) (SunTech Journal, Sun Microsystems, Vol. 3 No. 1, Winter 1990), with a [scan of the issue](/cv/suntech-1990.pdf).
- Honours thesis on a computer language for representing and processing music, ["Rubato: A Music Input and Performance System"](/cv/article/rubato/) (Basser Department of Computer Science, University of Sydney, 1987), for which I was awarded the University Medal in 1988.
