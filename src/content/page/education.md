I am a lifelong learner. Here is a summary of my formal education and academic achievements.

## Papers / Conferences

- Presented several times to the Tableau User Group in Sydney, and to R-Ladies Sydney. I have not been able to find copies of these presentations.
- Wrote Section 13.2 (Manage Portfolio, Program, and Project) of [APQC's Process Classification Framework](/cv/article/apqc/), which APQC adopted in Version 7.0.0 (October 2015); Version 6.0.0 credits Transfield Services alongside Intel and IBM.
- Presented ["Services Oriented Architecture: opportunities and challenges of embedding SOA in a Retail Bank"](/cv/article/banktech-2006/) at Bank.Tech.06 (Sydney, 27–28 July 2006), while Head of Architecture, Technology Australia, Retail Banking at NAB, with the [original deck](/cv/Banktech06%20NAB%20Chris%20Tham%20SOA.ppt).
- Presented ["The Role of Service Oriented Architecture within an Enterprise Architecture"](/cv/article/eac-2005/) at The Enterprise Architecture Conference 2005 (Sydney, 8–10 August 2005), while Head of Architecture, Distribution at NAB, with the [original deck](/cv/The%20Role%20of%20SOA%20within%20EA.ppt).
- Presented ["The promise and pitfalls of implementing a Services Oriented Architecture"](/cv/article/msac-2005/) to the Microsoft Architect Council (Sydney, 3 August 2005), with the [original deck](/cv/Microsoft%20Architect%20Council%20-%20The%20promise%20and%20pitfalls%20of%20implementing%20SOA.ppt).
- Sat on the panel at an ACS NSW Branch Forum on SOA (June 2005), part of the society's "Education Across the Nation" series. [Kate Behan's background presentation](/cv/article/acs-2005/), which sourced its definition and rationale from my earlier SOA talk, is reproduced here with her [original deck](/cv/SOA-%20ACS%20June%20Branch%20forum%20Presentation.ppt).
- Presented ["The promise and pitfalls of implementing a Services Oriented Architecture"](/cv/article/ark-2005/) at Ark Group's Planning and Implementing Service-Oriented Architecture conference (Sydney Marriott, 15–17 March 2005), while Head of Architecture, Distribution at NAB, with the [organisers' PDF](/cv/ark-soa-2005.pdf) and the [delegate feedback report](/cv/Implementing%20SOA%20-%20Speaker%20Feedback.pdf).
- Wrote four articles for APC magazine's September 2004 issue, while Head of Architecture at MLC: a [digital TV software roundup](/cv/article/apc-2004/) (also reproduced as [APC's edit of it](/cv/article/apc-2004-edit/)), a [myHTPC workshop](/cv/article/apc-2004-workshop/) and a [remote controls tutorial](/cv/article/apc-2004-remotes/).
- Presented ["Selling web services to senior management"](/cv/article/webservices-2004/) in July 2004, a case study of the architecture practice at MLC, while Head of Architecture there, with the [original deck](/cv/Selling%20web%20services%20to%20senior%20management.ppt). The deck names no conference and nothing survives that records which one it was.
- Presented ["Lessons Learnt from implementing and embedding an enterprise architecture team at National Wealth Management"](/cv/article/btell-2003/) at both BTELL Enterprise Architecture Conferences of 2003, in Sydney on 24 July and in Wellington over 4–6 November, while Manager, Architecture at National Wealth Management, with the [original deck](/cv/4%20Tham.ppt).
- Presented ["Getting technology right for advisers"](/cv/article/advisers-2003/) in September 2003, on behalf of Geoff Wenborn, then CIO of National Wealth Management — he could not attend and asked me to give it, and I had built the deck from his ideas. It names no conference; I believe it is the case study my 2005 résumé lists as "Case Study: National Wealth Management", given to the Open Group's Enterprise Architecture Special Interest Group in Sydney, but the deck itself does not say so. Reproduced from the [deck as delivered](/cv/GettingTechnologyRightforAdvisers.ppt), with [the earlier 9 September state](/cv/GettingTechnologyRightforAdvisers-9Sep2003.ppt) alongside it.
- Presented ["Electronic Commerce and the Consumer"](/cv/article/ecommerce-1997/) at the Finance Industry Reform conference (Sydney, 1997), while at Hewlett-Packard, on why consumers were hesitating over online shopping and what that meant for bank delivery channels. Reproduced from the [original deck](/cv/ECOMCON.PPT), with the [shorter later cut](/cv/ECOM.PPT) alongside it.
- Presented "Distributed Client/Server Architectures: What Are The Options" at the Client/Server Strategies Seminar (Sydney, 1995), while at AT&T Global Information Solutions — a third outing for the material behind the two 1994 papers. No copy has turned up.
- Presented "Distributed Client/Server Architectures and Transaction Processing on Open Systems" at [AUUG'94](/cv/article/auug-1994/) and, in revised form, at [Oracle OpenWorld '94](/cv/article/openworld-1994/), while at AT&T Global Information Solutions, with the Word manuscripts for [AUUG'94](/cv/DCSATP.DOC) and [OpenWorld](/cv/C_THAM.DOC).
- Presented ["Performance Tuning for Relational Database Applications"](/cv/article/auug-1993/) at AUUG'93, while at NCR Australia, with the [Word manuscript](/cv/AUUG.DOC).
- Co-authored technical paper ["UNIX Password Encryption considered insecure"](/cv/article/crypt-usenix91/) (USENIX Winter '91 Conference, Dallas, pp. 269–279), with a [scan of the proceedings](/cv/crypt-usenix91.pdf).
- Developed a method for distributed/parallel computation of options risk management, co-authored as ["Distributed Computing & Risk Management"](/cv/article/suntech-1990/) (SunTech Journal, Sun Microsystems, Vol. 3 No. 1, Winter 1990), with a [scan of the issue](/cv/suntech-1990.pdf) and [the longer manuscript we submitted](/cv/article/optech-1990/).
- Honours thesis on a computer language for representing and processing music, ["Rubato: A Music Input and Performance System"](/cv/article/rubato/) (Basser Department of Computer Science, University of Sydney, 1987), for which I was awarded the University Medal in 1988.
